This weekend was the [Minnesota Street Rod Association](https://msra.com/)'s annual "Back to the 50s" event. It's a huge classic car show with all kinds of vehicles. I am not super into old cars but my uncle is, and he was thrilled to have a captive audience as we walked around and he told me all about the different cars, which ones were his favorites, and why.

I learned that my favorite type of custom car is a "Rat Rod" - cars built on the cheap and intentionally meant to look "ratty." It's part of a counter-culture against overpriced customs built by people for the aesthetic and prestige of owning one instead of one that you actually drive. I think they look so awesome and raw and I would totally drive one of these.

![](/images/20260620-rat-rod.jpg)

![](/images/20260620-rat-rod-2.jpg)

If I ever built a custom car, I would make something like this. Only mine would be an Opossum Rod instead :)

The show being in town means there are way more classic cars on the road, which is really fun and makes errands much more exciting. I wish manufacturers still made cars that looked cool, everything these days is so boring and monochrome. I'm sure there's some way to make an aerodynamic and efficient car that also looks cool in 2026. Something like the [ID. Buzz](https://www.vw.com/en/models/id-buzz.html) is a step in the right direction in my opinion. If I wasn't explicitly looking for a small car, and also could afford one, I would really consider getting one of them. I've seen a couple on the road and they look awesome.
